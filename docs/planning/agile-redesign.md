Agile Redesign: Shelf

Problems with a Waterfall-style plan (from Assignment 2.1, Question 3)
No demos until the build phase is complete — violates early/continuous delivery and "working software as the primary measure of progress."
Requirements frozen once design begins — violates "welcome changing requirements, even late in development."
Design fully separated from build and from the people who'll use it — violates "business people and developers must work together daily."
One full QA pass and one full launch at the end — violates continuous testing and sustainable pace; concentrates all delivery risk into two all-or-nothing events.


Iterative redesign for Shelf
Iteration 1 (MVP, 1 week)

One board, three columns: Want to Read, Currently Reading, Finished.
Add a book (title + author); move it between columns.
No ratings, notes, or persistence yet just the core Board → Column → Card loop working end to end.
Demo: a real book moving through its lifecycle on the board.

Iteration 2 (1 week later)

Persist data between sessions.
Add rating + short notes when a card reaches Finished.
Edit/delete a card.
Demo: reading history from Iteration 1 is still there, plus a finished book with my rating and notes attached.

Each iteration delivers something usable immediately, instead of waiting on a frozen spec built out all at once.
